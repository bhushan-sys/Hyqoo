# Hyqoo Landing Page Plan

## Goal
Build a polished, single-page Hyqoo website for enterprise technology hiring. It will follow the supplied 11-section content brief closely and borrow n8n’s confident, technical presentation—dark immersive surfaces, precise interface-like details, strong editorial type, and restrained motion—without copying n8n’s branding or page composition.

## Page structure
1. **Navigation** — Hyqoo logo, the planned menu labels, and a visual-only “Hire talent” action.
2. **Hero: The Living Network** — approved headline and supporting copy beside an animated field of mint nodes narrowing from 14M+ professionals into a few matched profiles, finished with the gold Hyqoo spark.
3. **Trust** — one quiet, continuously moving monochrome client-name rail with the Collabera attribution. It will use typographic placeholders until official client logo files are supplied.
4. **The hiring problem** — four pressures presented over a widening demand-versus-availability data visual.
5. **The platform** — a polished simulated client interface with Match, Vet, and Hire & Manage states. The role field and controls will demonstrate the experience visually without submitting or navigating.
6. **AI + EI** — the signature “Mind + Heart” convergence: a precise machine lattice and organic human rhythm resolving into the gold four-point spark.
7. **Quality at scale** — scroll-led four-stage vetting funnel. The unresolved pass rate will remain visibly marked as `[X%]` rather than inventing a figure.
8. **Speed** — 24/48/72-hour progression compared with a traditional 2–3 month track, using the confirmed reach of 30+ countries across 5 continents.
9. **Breadth** — six technical domains rendered as network constellations, paired with the four engagement models.
10. **Why Hyqoo** — scale-versus-care comparison and the five differentiators from the brief.
11. **Proof and happier talent** — metric counters, featured case study, and an abstract mobility map. No invented testimonial or stock portrait will be added.
12. **Close, talent strip, and footer** — return to the living network, show both buyer actions as visual-only controls, route talent conceptually, and finish with the planned footer columns.

## Visual direction
- Use the supplied brand palette exactly: deep teal `#05262B`, gold `#AA9269`, mint `#6CBFB5`, and teal `#178888`, translated into semantic site colors.
- Preserve the brief’s color logic: deep teal as the ground, mint for networks and data, gold only for earned quality and key actions.
- Use the exact heading and body font links once supplied; do not use the font names suggested in the document as substitutes.
- Keep the n8n-inspired qualities that fit Hyqoo: dark technical atmosphere, compact bordered navigation, generous negative space, fine data lines, interface fragments, and measured high-contrast calls to action.
- Avoid purple AI gradients, generic stock developers, floating laptop mockups, decorative gold, generic domain icons, and copied n8n artwork.

## Motion and interaction
- Animate from many-to-few and scattered-to-resolved throughout the page.
- Add subtle node drift, narrowing funnels, count transitions, logo motion, interactive platform states, and restrained button feedback.
- Respect reduced-motion settings with clear static compositions and no loss of meaning.
- Keep every control in this first version visual-only, as requested; no extra pages, forms, or external links will be created.

## Responsive and quality checks
- Adapt every section for desktop and mobile, including stable data visuals and readable long-form copy.
- Keep a single page heading, meaningful landmarks, keyboard-friendly controls, descriptive labels, and suitable contrast.
- Add the supplied title and description metadata for the home page.
- Verify the full page in the live preview at desktop and mobile widths, including motion fallback, layout, and console errors.

## Inputs still needed
- Exact heading and body font links.
- Official Hyqoo logo file.

These can be supplied before or during implementation. Until received, the build will use a clearly isolated temporary wordmark and system fallback solely to keep layout work moving; both will be replaced before final sign-off.
